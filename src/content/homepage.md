Whether you’re looking to take your very first Balboa class, visiting New York for the weekend, or you're a local dancer looking for your next Bal-fix, you’ve come to the right place.

NYC Balboa is your one-stop resource for all things Balboa in New York City. Find where to dance, where to learn, who’s teaching, and what’s happening in the community. Browse the [Calendar](/calendar) for upcoming events, explore [Learn](/learn) for classes and practice sessions, and visit [Resources](/resources) to dive deeper into the history and music that makes Balboa so special.

Balboa is a close-embrace swing dance that originated in Southern California during the 1930s and 40s. Today, NYC is home to a welcoming community of dancers who love sharing it.

We think it’s a pretty great scene. Come dance with us.
